SmartInsights Demo Store Business Policy

Return Policy:
Customers may return defective electronics within 7 days of delivery with original packaging.

Shipping Policy:
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery is available in metro cities.

Inventory Policy:
Any product at or below reorder level must be replenished within 48 hours.

Customer Service Goals:
Respond to complaints within 24 hours. Delivery delay complaints should be escalated to logistics.
